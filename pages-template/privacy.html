Privacy Notice · Effective August 2026

Privacy, in plain language.

Kontrol is a Windows application that connects hardware controls to supported games. This notice explains the limited technical telemetry used when Kontrol checks licenses and verifies adapter updates.

What is Collected

The licensing service stores a pseudonymous device-binding value derived locally from hardware information. Raw hardware identifiers are never sent. It also records client version numbers, edge country codes, and access timestamps.

Why it is Needed

This data enforces license binding, protects services against abuse, ensures safe crash diagnostics, and assists customer support. Kontrol never transmits in-game flight telemetry, raw keystrokes, or controller binding files.

Administrative Records

License issuance, lookup, unbinding, and administrative actions are logged with pseudonymous audit identifiers. Raw activation keys, passwords, IP addresses, and browser user-agents are excluded from permanent logs.

Data Retention

Activation and security audit logs are retained for 90 days. Basic license status records are maintained during active service to permit re-activations and support history.

Your Choices & Support

You can request data lookup, license unbinding, or report issues through the official Kontrol Adapters Issue Tracker. For security disclosures, refer to SECURITY.md.

Updates to this Notice

Whenever data practices or licensing architecture materially change, this document will be updated with an updated revision date.
