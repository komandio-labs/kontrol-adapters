# Third-party notices

This repository uses third-party packages through NuGet. Their applicable
licenses must be included with every adapter package that redistributes their
runtime binaries. The Space Engineers 2 package carries its concrete Harmony
notice in `src/Adapters/SpaceEngineers2/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Space Engineers 2 and VRage assemblies are game-owned files. They are not
included in this repository or in adapter packages.
