Privacy notice · effective 15 August 2026

Privacy, in plain language.

Kontrol is a Windows application that lets supported games use additional input devices. This notice explains the limited technical data used when Kontrol activates a license and checks its status.

What is collected

The licensing service stores a pseudonymous device-binding value derived on the computer from local hardware information. The raw hardware identifiers are not sent. It also stores the Kontrol version, a bounded client descriptor, a country code supplied by the edge service, and activation, access, and revocation timestamps.

Why it is needed

This data is used to activate and limit a license to its permitted device, detect abuse, keep the service secure, provide support, and enforce revocations. The application does not send game files, input values, or profile contents to the licensing service.

Administrative records

License issuance, export, lookup, unbinding, and revocation actions are recorded using a pseudonymous administrator fingerprint. Raw activation keys, certificates, authorization tokens, raw IP addresses, hostnames, and raw browser user-agent strings are not stored.

Retention

Activation and administrative audit events are retained for 90 days. License and device-binding records are retained while needed to enforce an active or revoked license and its support history. Abuse-control records follow the same operational retention process.

Your choices and questions

You can stop using Kontrol and request support or ask a privacy question through the Kontrol adapters issue tracker. Please do not include an activation key, certificate, or device identifier in a public issue. For security reports, use the instructions in SECURITY.md.

Changes to this notice

When the licensing data or retention practices materially change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
